Settings and Users on a laptop

Today both pages sit in the 480 px column of main.narrow at every screen size, so a laptop shows the phone layout. The library uses up to 1200 px. These mockups give Settings and Users the same 1200 px from 900 px up; under 900 px they stay as they are today, and the sign-in page keeps its narrow card.

Two layouts for Settings, A and B, each in the Soft and the E-ink look, and one layout for Users. Each frame is 1320 px wide.

Theme

ASettings as rows

Each section is a row: its heading and explanation on the left, its fields and buttons on the right, with a line between rows. Fields stay at most 520 px wide. In Soft the whole page is one card, as today.

BSettings as cards

Each section is a card of its own, in two columns. Heading, explanation and fields stay stacked as on the phone. In Soft the cards float on the page background; in E-ink they are boxes with the 2 px line.
